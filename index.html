What is this Conway's Game of Life guff all about?

It's an example of a cellular automaton.

Cellular automata are simple rules-based simulations in which cells can be alive or dead, and on each 'tick' of the simulation a cell decides whether it stays alive, dead, or switches between states based on what its neighbours are doing.

If you want to read more about how they work, click here.
